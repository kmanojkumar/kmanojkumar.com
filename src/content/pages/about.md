## 🧑‍💻 About me

**CS Engineer | Entrepreneur | Founder and CEO, VegaStack**

Hey there! I am a **CS Engineer** and **Entrepreneur**, currently based in
Bengaluru 🇮🇳.

I began my career in the public sector at the Ministry of HRD, Government of
Andhra Pradesh, where I developed e-learning solutions for State Govt.
universities. I then moved on to creating deep-tech solutions for companies
like PwC, NIIT, and Aricent. I've built software products in a bunch of
diverse domains and have been tinkering with **Linux, DevOps, Security, and
Process Automation** for a while now.

At the age of **19**, while pursuing Engineering, I founded **PeerXP** (now VegaStack)! Since
2019, I have been assisting growth-stage businesses with their **DevSecOps and
Software Delivery challenges**, enabling them to scale faster by leveraging Tech
Automation.
